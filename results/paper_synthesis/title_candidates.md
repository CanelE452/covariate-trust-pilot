# Title candidates

No title is final. No candidate uses "state of the art", "universal", "optimal", "always" or
"first".

---

**T1. When Does Factorizing Intermittent Demand Help? A Controlled Study of Temporal Dependence
and Its Empirical Boundary**

Strength — names the question, the method and the fact that there is a boundary. "Boundary"
signals the honest half without sounding like a negative-results paper.
Weakness — long, and "factorizing" may read as matrix factorization to a skim reader.
**Rank 1.**

---

**T2. Occurrence Structure, Not Sparsity: Conditional Inductive Bias in Direct and Hurdle
Forecasts of Intermittent Demand**

Strength — the strongest single sentence of positioning; makes the ADI/CV² contrast the hook.
Weakness — "Not sparsity" is an overclaim against the evidence, since the adjusted partial
association contains zero and H3 was not tested at its intended contrast. Would need to become
"Occurrence Structure Beyond Sparsity".
**Rank 3** as written, **Rank 2** if softened.

---

**T3. A Conditional Advantage That Does Not Transfer: Point and Hurdle Forecasting for
Intermittent Demand**

Strength — accurate and memorable.
Weakness — reads as a pure negative-results paper and undersells C2, where the selector does
transfer across seeds and partially across datasets. It would invite desk rejection at venues
that undervalue negative results.
**Rank 5.**

---

**T4. From Controlled Contrast to Real Demand: Validating and Bounding a Structural Rule for
Intermittent-Demand Factorization**

Strength — describes the actual arc of the paper, including the freeze-then-test discipline.
Weakness — "structural rule" is vague until Section 5; no mention of routing.
**Rank 4.**

---

**T5. Temporal Dependence and the Choice Between Direct and Factorized Intermittent-Demand
Forecasts: A Controlled Study with an Adaptive-Routing Boundary**

Strength — covers all three contributions and sets expectations so the routing section is not
read as a failed method.
Weakness — the longest of the five; the subtitle does the work the abstract should.
**Rank 2.**

---

## Recommendation

Take **T1**, with T5 as the fallback if the venue prefers explicit scope in the title. If T2 is
preferred for its sharper framing, it must be changed to "Beyond Sparsity" — "Not Sparsity"
asserts something the artifacts do not support.
